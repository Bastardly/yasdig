Generate small and fast, keep the good ones, then Hi-res, Refine, Edit, Extend or Upscale just those — each step one you can undo.

One file, no Python

Nothing to install. Runs on AMD, NVIDIA and Intel graphics cards through Vulkan, powered by stable-diffusion.cpp.

Models in one click

SDXL, Flux, Flux.2 Klein, Z-Image, Krea 2, Qwen-Image and more — downloaded to the right place from inside the app.

Getting started

  1. Download the file for your system, above.
  2. Put it in a folder of its own and start it. Your browser opens on the app.
  3. Open Get models, download a model, then press Generate.

You need a graphics card with a current driver (AMD, NVIDIA or Intel). How much memory depends on the model; the app says per model.

On Linux, make the file executable once: chmod +x yasdig-linux-x86_64. On Windows, a new download may show Windows protected your PC: choose More info → Run anyway.

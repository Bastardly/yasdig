Image

Most generators make you pick the expensive settings before you know whether the picture is any good. YASDIG turns that round: draft small and fast, keep the good ones, and spend GPU time only on those.

A workspace, not a form

A rail down the left opens floating panels over the picture — Prompts, Models, Generation, Image to image, Hi-res pass, Refine pass, Info, Current and Favorites. Drag each where you want it; it stays there next time. A panel that departs from a plain generate says so in its title, and its rail button carries a dot even while it's closed.

Batches and loops

Set Images to four and press Generate 4: each picture appears as it lands. Tick Until stopped and it keeps going — and a loop follows the panel, so change the prompt or the model while it runs and the next image uses it.

Every image kept, with what made it

Each generation lands in the work-item grid with its prompt, seed, model and every setting, so any one can be reopened and taken further later. Star the ones you love: favourites survive Delete all. Saved PNGs carry their settings in a form A1111, Forge and Civitai read.

Then escalate just the keepers

Hi-res makes a picture bigger and adds the detail a plain upscale can't invent; Refine goes over it lightly. Both can use a different model from the draft — draft on a fast Turbo model, finish on a detailed one. A click on the picture opens Refine & adjust for the rest.

Prompt tools

  • Weights: Ctrl+↑/↓ or Ctrl+wheel on a word writes (word:1.2).
  • {variables} roll a different word for each image of a batch.
  • Enhance writes your few words out in full for the model you picked — tags for SDXL, sentences for the rest — and Ctrl+Z puts yours back.
  • References: the Klein models look at up to four pictures while they draw (“the jacket from image 2”).
  • Image to image: start from your own picture — upload, drop or paste it.
  • LoRAs, checked against the model, so one made for another family is refused rather than silently doing nothing.
  • Layout boxes on MOSS-Image: say where each thing and each line of text goes in the frame.

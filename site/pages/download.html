Download

Free and open source, for Windows and Linux PCs.

Getting started

  1. Download the file for your system, above.
  2. Extract it somewhere you can write to — Documents, say, not Program Files. On Windows, right-click the zip → Extract All. You get a yasdig folder: keep it, everything the app makes goes in there.
  3. Start yasdig in that folder. Your browser opens on the app.
  4. Open Models, download a model, then press Generate.

On Windows, the first start may show Windows protected your PC: choose More info → Run anyway. YASDIG is open source but not yet signed with a Microsoft certificate.

What you need

  • A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 or Linux PC. There is no macOS version.
  • A graphics card with a current driver — AMD, NVIDIA or Intel. Current drivers all include Vulkan, which is what YASDIG draws with.
  • Graphics memory to suit the model: the Models page lists what each takes. With less, the app can keep a model in system RAM or stream it from the SSD.

Developed and tested on Linux with an AMD Radeon RX 7900 XTX (24 GB). Other Vulkan GPUs and Windows should work; reports are welcome.

Updating and stopping

To stop it, close its console window or press Close server on the page. To update, replace yasdig.exe (or yasdig) with the one from the new archive: your models, settings and pictures stay where they are.

yasdig(.exe)       the program
models/            the models you download
user/              your settings, favourites and cast — never cleared
work/              every generation, and work/output/ for the pictures you save
bin/               stable-diffusion.cpp, unpacked from the program

Privacy and checking the download

Everything stays on your computer. No account, no telemetry, no update check. YASDIG goes online only when you press download on the Models tab, and then only to Hugging Face. The page it opens listens on 127.0.0.1, so other machines on your network can't reach it.

Models load in a sandbox with no network, on Linux and on Windows: even a crafted model file that broke in couldn't send anything anywhere.

Each release can be checked three ways, all shown when you press a download button: VirusTotal's scan (Linux), the SHA-256, and GitHub's attestation that it built the archive from the public source.

Assistant

A chat with a small language model that runs on your own GPU, the way the image models do — on the Image and Video tabs' rails. Nothing goes to the cloud; the 4B model fits an 8 GB card.

Talk about prompts, and draw

Ask for a prompt, a rewrite or ideas — it can change the tab's settings as it answers, each change a line with an Undo. Say “draw a fox in a red hat” or “three of those” and it writes the prompt and draws them.

Describe a picture

Attach a picture and ask for it as a prompt. It writes one in the style of the chat's image model — tags for SD 1.x and SDXL, sentences for the rest — with Copy to the Image tab.

Improve, round by round

“Keep trying until the hat is red”, or attach a picture and say “find the best prompt for this”. The agent proposes a session first and draws nothing until you press Start. Then it writes yes/no checks, draws a round, judges every picture, changes one or two settings — only the ones you allow — and goes again, until a picture passes or the rounds run out.

Compare settings

“What's the best CFG for this?” “Which model draws hands better?” It draws one round per value on the same seeds, so the value is the only difference, and answers from what it sees.

Films and the board

On the Video tab, the Assistant writes short films as storyboards — shots, cast and keyframes — that become the Video tab's shots.

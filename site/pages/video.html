Video

The Video tab is laid out like the Image tab: a rail of panels, the clip filling the rest. Text or a first frame to video, with LoRAs and a live preview while it renders.

The models

  • Wan 2.2 — TI2V-5B, and the I2V-A14B pair; Wan 2.1 VACE and dense models.
  • LTX-2.3 and LTX-2.5 — picture and soundtrack together at 24 fps; distilled, 8 steps, with LTX's own ×2 hi-res in the same run.
  • MiniMax H3 — with sound, and an 8-step Turbo LoRA.

The Models tab has one-click cards for them.

A clip is shots

A model this size runs out of memory a few seconds in, so a longer clip is made a shot at a time: each shot has its own prompt, seed and length, and goes on from the previous shot's last frame. What every shot shares — the characters, the place, the look — goes in Every shot. Five shots of three seconds make a fifteen-second clip, up to twenty shots.

Enlarge without flicker

Enlarge runs FSR over every frame — fixed filters, so nothing flickers. With an LTX model and its latent upscaler installed, LTX can instead redraw each shot at twice its size, Wan's and H3's clips too: it sees all of a shot's frames at once, so the new detail holds still. Clips save to Outputs as video files.

Narrated stills

Your pictures with a voice reading over them, in the voice of a short recording you pick — Chatterbox (English, or 23 languages) or Qwen3-TTS.

Films from a storyboard

The Assistant writes a short film as a storyboard, with your cast in it, and the storyboard becomes the Video tab's shots.

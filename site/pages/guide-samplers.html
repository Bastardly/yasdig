Samplers

The sampler is how each step removes noise. At enough steps most give a similar picture; what changes is the look (crisp, soft, textured), how few steps they need, and whether the same seed gives the same picture.

Start with what the model fills in. Turbo, Lightning, LCM and the newer models (Flux, Z-Image, Qwen-Image) are trained for one sampler and look wrong on others.

Good starting points

ModelSampler
Realistic and photo SDXLdpm++2m_sde, 30 steps — or dpm++2m, 25 steps. With karras
Anime and illustration SDXLeuler_a, 25–30 steps
SD 1.5dpm++2m + karras, 20–25 steps
SDXL Turbo, Lightning, Hypereuler, the steps on the model’s card (4–8), CFG 1–2
Anything with an LCM LoRAlcm, 4–8 steps, CFG 1–2
Flux, FLUX.3, Krea 2, Qwen-Image, Z-Imageeuler
Quick drafts on SDXLipndm, 12–15 steps

Repeatable

Same seed, same picture. More steps refine the image rather than change it.

SamplerWhat you’ll see
eulerClean and neutral. The safe first try on any model.
dpm++2mCrisp and detailed by 20–25 steps. The usual pick for realistic SDXL and SD 1.5.
dpm++2mv2Like dpm++2m, a little cleaner at low step counts.
ipndm, ipndm_vHolds together at 10–15 steps, where euler is still blurry. Good for drafts.
heun, dpm2Slightly cleaner edges than euler. Twice as slow per step — use half the steps.
res_multistepSharper than euler at the same steps. Worth a try when a Flux or Qwen-Image looks soft.
res_2sAs above, a bit sharper again. Twice as slow per step.
ddim_trailingOnly when a model’s card asks for DDIM.
lmsAn older method. No reason to pick it today.

Varying

Names ending in _a or sde add a little fresh noise each step. The picture keeps changing as you add steps, and seeds differ more from each other.

SamplerWhat you’ll see
euler_aSofter and smoother, a painterly look. Loved for anime and illustration; can blur fine detail in photos.
dpm++2m_sdeMore fine texture: skin pores, fabric, film grain. Needs 25+ steps. Great for realistic portraits.
dpm++2m_sde_btLike dpm++2m_sde, a slightly different texture. Try both.
er_sdeTextured like the sde ones, but already good at 15–20 steps.
dpm++2s_aDetailed with a soft finish. Twice as slow per step.

Few-step

Only for models or LoRAs trained for them; on a normal model they look washed out.

SamplerWhat you’ll see
lcmSmooth, a bit waxy. For LCM models and LoRAs at 4–8 steps, CFG 1–2.
tcdLike lcm with more detail kept. For TCD LoRAs at 4–8 steps.

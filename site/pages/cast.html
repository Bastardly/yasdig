Cast

Keep someone or something under a name — a character, a place, a prop — so a picture or a clip can have them again.

A name, a look, a picture

Write the look in words — “Elias is a weathered cowboy in his late 40s…” — and press Draw. On a model that follows reference pictures (the Kleins), a character gets a turnaround sheet first — front, three-quarter, side and back — and the picture is drawn from it, so the two are one person. Or use a picture of your own.

A voice

A character can have a voice, from a short recording you pick — heard in narrated stills and in films.

Name them anywhere

Name a cast member in a prompt, in a clip's shots or in a film, and their look and picture go with them. Cast entries live in your own folder, which nothing sweeps: clearing the work never reaches them.

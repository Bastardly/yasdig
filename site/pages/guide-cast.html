Cast

The Cast tab keeps characters, places and props under a name, to have them again in other pictures and clips. New cast… opens the editor: a name, how they look in words, and a picture drawn from it.

Write the look starting with the name, then what a stranger would need to draw it:

Name: Elias
"Elias is a weathered cowboy in his late 40s, with a lean but strong build, a grey stubble and a sun-bleached leather hat."

Then add them in the Prompts panel's Cast row and use the name in the prompt: "Elias lights a cigar while looking at Sally." Words puts their look before your prompt; Picture (Kleins only) makes their picture a reference the model follows.

Draw again keeps every try in a strip; Save keeps the one you picked. On a Klein a character gets a turnaround sheet first, and the picture is drawn from it. Save as cast… in the Refine & adjust dialog starts an entry from the picture you are looking at.

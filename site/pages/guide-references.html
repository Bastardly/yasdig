References

Pictures the model looks at while it draws, so it can keep a person, a piece of clothing or a place from your own images. With a model that takes them — Flux.2 Klein and FLUX.3 Klein for now — a References button appears in the Generation section. Pick up to four images there (Choose, Upload, drag one in, or paste), then name them in the prompt by number:

"the woman from image 1 wearing the jacket from image 2, walking through a market"

Each reference costs about as much as the image itself, so four make a generation noticeably slower; large photos are shrunk to the model’s size first. The picks stay when you switch to a model that doesn’t use them, and come back when you switch back. Reopening a work item puts its references back in their slots.

To change an image you already have, use Edit in the Refine & adjust dialog: it uses the image itself as reference 1.

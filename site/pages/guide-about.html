About & licences

yasdig is Yet Another Stable Diffusion Image Generator — a tip of the hat to the open-source habit of calling a thing "yet another" one, and to the many generators before it. It was called rustimage until October 2026.

It is free software under the Apache License 2.0.

It is powered by stable-diffusion.cpp (MIT licence), which this program carries inside itself and runs to draw every picture, and by Vulkan, which your graphics driver provides.

Models

Models are not part of yasdig. Every model has its own licence, set by the people who made it: some allow anything, some forbid commercial use of what you make. Check a model’s page before using its pictures — each card on the Models tab links the pages its files come from.

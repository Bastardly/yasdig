Schedulers

The scheduler decides how much noise is left after each step — where the steps are spent. Spending more of them near the end, where little noise is left, tends to give cleaner fine detail.

Leave it on Auto unless you have a reason. Auto is not one scheduler: sd.cpp picks per model — Flux and Flux.2 get schedules of their own, lcm and tcd get lcm, ddim_trailing gets simple, and everything else gets discrete. Forcing discrete onto a Flux makes it worse.

SchedulerWhat it does
AutoThe model’s own choice, as above. The default.
discreteThe noise levels the model was trained on, evenly spaced. What Auto picks for SD and SDXL.
karrasPacks more steps into the low-noise end, for cleaner fine detail. The classic partner for DPM++ samplers.
exponentialEvenly spaced on a log scale. Similar in spirit to karras, a different spread.
sgm_uniformUniform spacing that ends exactly at zero noise. Common with distilled, few-step models.
simpleA plain even pick from the model’s noise levels.
ays“Align Your Steps”: schedules tuned for very few steps. SD 1.5 and SDXL only — refused for other models.
lcmFor LCM-distilled models. Auto picks it for the lcm and tcd samplers.

More

SchedulerWhat it does
gitsPrecomputed few-step schedules, like ays.
smoothstepAn S-curve: slow at both ends, quick in the middle.
kl_optimalSpacing derived to minimise error per step; good at low step counts.
betaSpends steps at both ends, little in the middle. A common choice in ComfyUI workflows.
bong_tangentA tangent-shaped curve with a similar both-ends shape. For experimenting.
flux, flux2Flux’s and Flux.2’s own schedules — what Auto already picks for them.

MiniMax H3 films sound with the picture: speech, the sounds of the place, and any score. It fills whatever a shot leaves open, with sentences nobody wrote, so leave nothing open. The server writes your fields into H3's own layout and adds who stays silent; write plain words, no tags.

A shot's `text` is what is seen, never what is said: no quotes, no "she says". It covers the whole shot: what happens after the last line, done silently ("she sets the cup down without a word").

`lines`, what is spoken, in order:
- Only lines the story has. Nobody talks to fill a silence or explain what we see.
- `who`: the speaker's cast name exactly, or `narrator` only when the person asked for one.
- `words`: word for word; the person's words kept exactly, in their language, which `language` names. One language per shot.
- `voiceover`: true when a cast member is heard but doesn't speak on screen (thoughts, a letter read).
- Time it: about two words a second, with room to spare. A line too long for the shot is split over two shots.

`sound`: one or two sentences of what is heard besides speech: the place (rain on a roof, room tone, traffic) and what is done (footsteps, a door, a cup set down). Name the sources, not a mood. Never empty while anyone is on screen: a quiet stretch with nothing named is where H3 mumbles. Empty only for complete silence.

`music`: a score only the audience hears. Empty unless the person asked for music; then the instruments and tempo ("Slow solo piano, sparse notes"). Music people in the shot hear (a radio, singing) goes in `text` and `sound`.

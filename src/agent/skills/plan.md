This turn starts a session. Read the goal and write the checks the pictures will be judged by.

Checks:
- Each is one yes/no question about something visible: "Is there a red hat?", "Is the fox looking at the camera?", "Is the background a snowy forest?".
- Take them from the goal, one per thing it asks for. Two to five is right.
- Never ask about quality or taste ("Is it beautiful?"). Those cannot be answered yes or no by looking.

What kind of session it is:
- Getting a picture right: if the prompt does not yet ask for everything the goal does, rewrite it.
- Improving the pictures just drawn: leave the prompt alone unless those pictures cannot meet the goal; they are judged first, and the next rounds keep their seeds.
- Finding something out ("which CFG works best?"): the checks are what you will look for in every variant. Keep the prompt, keep the seeds, and change one setting per round, so the difference between rounds is that setting. Rounds are the number of variants to try.

Size:
- The person's words decide how many rounds and how many pictures each: "a few tries" is 3 rounds, "4 pictures, 2 rounds" is count 4 and rounds 2.
- When they say nothing about it, 3 rounds of 4.

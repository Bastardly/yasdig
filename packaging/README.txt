YASDIG — Yet Another Stable Diffusion Image Generator
https://yasdig.com  ·  source: https://github.com/Bastardly/yasdig

Keep this folder. Everything YASDIG makes — models, settings, pictures — goes
in here, beside the program. Put the folder wherever you like (Documents, say,
not Program Files) and move it as a whole.

Start it:
  Windows  double-click yasdig.exe. Your browser opens on the app.
  Linux    run ./yasdig (or open it from your file manager).

Then open the Get models tab, download a model, and press Generate.

On Windows, the first start may show "Windows protected your PC". YASDIG is
free and open source, but not yet signed with a paid Microsoft certificate,
so Windows doesn't know it. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

To update, replace yasdig.exe (or yasdig) with the new one. Your models,
settings and pictures stay where they are.
